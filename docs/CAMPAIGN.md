# Werk Campaign

## North Star

> Compose rhythm through musical conversation.

Werk is not three drum machines running at once. It is three rhythmic musicians listening to one another.

The user directs character, role, form and intention. The engines create the rhythmic detail.

## Fixed Roles

- **Slot A — Primary Groove** establishes the rhythmic spine.
- **Slot B — Counter-Rhythm** challenges, enriches and converses with that spine.
- **Slot C — Structural Punctuation** marks arrivals, departures, transitions and form.

All three roles may use kicks, snares, hats, toms and percussion. Overlap is allowed and desirable when it creates musical interaction. Sound design and mapping make layered voices work together.

## Campaign Principles

1. **Emergence over note programming.**
2. **Conversation over isolated generation.**
3. **Form over endless jamming.**
4. **Musical language over algorithmic jargon.**
5. **Immediate mode without improvised geometry.**
6. **No GFX YAGNI or premature abstraction.**
7. **No embarrassing UI.**
8. **Accessibility is ordinary instrument design.**
9. **Every victory produces a playable creative outcome.**

# Victories

## Victory I — Werk Exists

One coherent JSFX instrument hosts three selectable Werk engines in the fixed roles.

**Creative result:** the user can hear three distinct rhythmic personalities together inside one instrument.

## Victory II — The Same Engine Learns Its Role

Role changes behaviour.

PulseWerk in Primary Groove behaves differently from PulseWerk in Counter-Rhythm. MetalWerk in Counter-Rhythm behaves differently from MetalWerk in Primary Groove.

**Creative result:** swapping engines and roles produces meaningfully different conversations, not parameter variations of the same pattern.

## Victory III — Sixteen Meaningful Pads

Werk speaks through sixteen semantic drum voices rather than hard-coded MIDI notes.

**Creative result:** one rhythmic arrangement can drive different drum instruments without rewriting the engines.

## Victory IV — REAPER Knows the Kit

Werk reads REAPER MIDI note-name map files and resolves semantic voices to the chosen kit.

**Creative result:** existing REAPER drum mappings become immediately useful inside Werk.

## Victory V — Form Becomes First-Class

The user can shape a complete rhythmic journey through sections, participation, energy, variation and transition behaviour.

**Creative result:** Werk produces an intentional beginning, development and ending rather than an indefinitely repeating jam.

## Victory VI — Three Musicians Listen

Engines publish musical state and respond to one another: sparse, busy, building, resting, filling, resolving and creating tension.

The conductor supports awareness rather than blunt collision avoidance.

**Creative result:** adding an engine creates a better conversation, not merely more notes.

## Victory VII — Werk Looks Like an Instrument

Werk's bespoke immediate-mode interface is spacious, deliberate and visually competitive with modern plug-ins.

It opens at a correct calculated size, measures text, respects rectangles, clips deliberately and resizes predictably.

**Creative result:** the interface feels trustworthy and inviting before the first note plays.

## Victory VIII — Werk Is Accessible

Werk supports scalable presentation, large targets, visible focus, keyboard use, alternatives to dragging, non-colour-only meaning, readable language and reduced-motion behaviour.

**Creative result:** beginners and differently abled producers can operate the instrument without fighting it.

## Victory IX — Jam Becomes Composition

Performance exploration can be captured and developed through Form into an editable rhythmic arrangement.

**Creative result:** a compelling live interaction becomes a song-level drum performance.

## Victory X — Werk Works With HIT

HIT communicates structure and intention. Werk interprets those requests through its three roles and returns editable rhythmic material.

**Creative result:** a HIT composition gains drums that support its journey without HIT programming individual notes.

## Final Victory — A Finished Work

Werk contributes the rhythmic composition to a completed Theory of Mine piece, developed through HIT and ultimately paired with Afterimage visuals for publication.

# Parallel GFX Capability

Werk is the first implementation of the immediate-mode GFX approach. There is no separate framework campaign.

Build concrete Werk UI. Extract only helpers proven by current repetition and pain. Spin out a library only when the reusable boundary has emerged through real use and extraction is clearly cheaper than leaving it in Werk.

The question is never:

> What generic UI framework should we build?

It is:

> What does Werk need to be correct, beautiful and accessible in this victory?
